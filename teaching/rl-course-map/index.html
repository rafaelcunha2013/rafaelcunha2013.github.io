Reinforcement Learning

An introductory course in eight lectures, with video recordings, tutorials and exercises, following Sutton and Barto's textbook.

The course goes from the exploration–exploitation dilemma in multi-armed bandits, through finite Markov decision processes, dynamic programming, and Monte Carlo and temporal-difference learning, to function approximation, policy gradients and model-based methods. It follows R. S. Sutton and A. G. Barto, Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction, 2nd ed., 2018, free online from the authors.
