Consider a random student from a class doing the Reinforcement Learning course and the probabilities \(\mathbb{P}(\text{the student will pass the course})\) and \(\mathbb{P}\)(the student will study a lot and will pass the course). Which one is more likely? Give a mathematical argument.

Hint: The answer might be counterintuitive. Consider \(\mathbb{P}(A \mid B)=\frac{\mathbb{P}(A , B)}{\mathbb{P}(B)}\) and remember that probabilities take values in \([0,1]\).
