State in words if the following converge or diverge. If they converge, find the limit informally. For the last one, it is enough to say whether it converges or not.

  • \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty 1\)

  • \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\)

  • \(\sum_{n=1}^{100} 1\)

  • \(\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{2^n}\)

  • \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{2^n}\)

  • \(\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n}\)

  • \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^{0.001}}\)

  • \(\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n^3}\)
