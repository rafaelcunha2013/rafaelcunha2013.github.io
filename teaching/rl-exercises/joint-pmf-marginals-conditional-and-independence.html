Consider the discrete random variables \(X\) and \(Y\) that take values 0 and 1 with a joint pmf:

Y=0 Y=1
X=0 0.25 0.4
X=1 0.3 0.05

Find the marginals \(\mathbb{P}(X), \mathbb{P}(Y)\), the probability \(\mathbb{P}(X=1, Y=0)\), the conditional probability \(\mathbb{P}(X=1 \mid Y=0)\) and decide whether \(X\) and \(Y\) are independent.
