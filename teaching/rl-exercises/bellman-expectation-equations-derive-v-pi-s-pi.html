(2 points) Derive the relation between \(q_{\pi}(s,a)\) and \(v_{\pi}(s)\), namely that \[\begin{align*} v_{\pi}(s) = \sum_a \pi(a|s) q_\pi(s,a). \end{align*}\] It might be helpful to start from \(\mathbb{E}(X|Y=y)=\sum_x x \times \mathbb{P}(X=x|Y=y)\) and \(\pi(a|s)=\mathbb{P}(a|s)\), then apply the definition of expectation and conditional probability.
