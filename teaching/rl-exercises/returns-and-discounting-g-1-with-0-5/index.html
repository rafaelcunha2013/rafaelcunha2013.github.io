Exercise 2.3.1

Returns and discounting: G_1 with γ = 0.5 and five rewards

Reinforcement Learning › Exercises › Lecture 2 · The RL Problem and Finite MDPs › 2.3 Returns and discounting
EasyMultiple choice

Suppose a discount factor \(\gamma = 0.5\) and we observe the following sequence of rewards: \(R_1 = -3, R_2 = 1, R_3 = 4, R_4 = 7\), and \(R_5 = 2\), with \(T = 5\), where \(T\) is the time-step representing the terminal state. What is \(G_1\), where \(G_t\) is the discounted return at time \(t\)? Recall that the discounted return \(G_t\) at time \(t\) is defined as the sum of discounted rewards received after time \(t\), mathematically represented as: \[\begin{equation*} G_t = R_{t+1} + \gamma R_{t+2} + \gamma^2 R_{t+3} + \cdots + \gamma^{T-1} R_{T} \end{equation*}\] where \(R_{t+k}\) is the reward received at time \(t+k\), \(k \in \{1, \ldots, T-t\}\), and \(\gamma \in [0,1]\) is the discount factor.
