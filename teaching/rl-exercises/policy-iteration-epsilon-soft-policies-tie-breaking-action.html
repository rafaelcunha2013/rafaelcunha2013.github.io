Consider the policy iteration algorithm for estimating \(\pi \approx \pi_*\).

  • (a) Suppose you are restricted to considering only policies that are \(\epsilon\)-soft, meaning that the probability of selecting each action in each state, \(s\), is at least \(\epsilon/|\mathcal{A}(s)|\). Describe qualitatively the changed that would be required in each steps 3,2, and 1, in that order, of the policy iteration algorithm for \(v_*\).

  • (b) The above policy iteration algorithm has a subtle bug in that it may never terminate if the policy continually switches between two or more policies that are equally good. Modify the pseudocode so that convergence is guaranteed.

  • (c) How would policy iteration be defined for action values? Give a complete algorithm for computing \(q_*\), analogous to the pseudocode above for computing \(v_*\).
