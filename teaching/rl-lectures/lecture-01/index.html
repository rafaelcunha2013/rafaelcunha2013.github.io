Probability Mass Function (PMF)

[Review]

•A function is a valid PMF if it follows two simple rules:

​

1.Rule 1: All outputs are valid probabilities.

​

​

​

2.Rule 2: The total probability is 1.

​

​

​

•A PMF describes the probability for each possible outcome of a
discrete random variable.

A Policy is a PMF over actions

•A policy is simply a PMF where the "outcomes" are
the possible actions the agent can take.

​

•A policy must follow the same two rules:

​

Rule 1 Applied to Policies: The probability of selecting any
single action `a` must be between 0 and 1.

​

​

​

​

Rule 2 Applied to Policies: The probabilities of selecting all
possible actions must sum to 1 (the agent has to choose
something).
